## Detailed description

Applywise is your AI job application copilot. Upload your resume once — our AI builds a complete profile with experience, education, and skills. Then autofill any job application in seconds.

**What you get:**
- **One-click autofill** on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed India, Greenhouse, Lever, and dozens more
- **AI resume parsing** — multi-pass extraction with quality scoring
- **Profile sync** with the Applywise web dashboard
- **Cover letter generation** tailored to the job you're viewing
- **LinkedIn networking** — import contacts and draft referral messages

**How it works:**
1. Sign in with Google (same account as applywise.app)
2. Upload your resume PDF
3. Open any job application page
4. Click **Autofill This Page**

**Privacy:** We use your profile only to power autofill and AI features. We do not sell your data. See our privacy policy on the Applywise website.

**Free to use** — core autofill and profile features are free. AI-heavy actions use daily free credits.
